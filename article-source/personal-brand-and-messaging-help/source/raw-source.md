## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the three C's of personal branding?

The three C's of personal branding stand for Clarity, Consistency, and Constancy. Clarity ensures your target audience understands exactly who you are, what problems you solve, and what sets your approach apart from standard market options.

Consistency means your verbal messaging, visual identity, and professional standards stay unified across every channel. Constancy means showing up reliably over time, reinforcing your core perspectives so you stay top of mind with key decision-makers.

### What are the 5 P's of personal branding?

The 5 P's of personal branding comprise Purpose, Positioning, Persona, Products, and Promotion. Purpose defines the underlying mission behind your ventures, while Positioning separates your perspective from typical industry competitors.

Persona guides how your natural style and voice show up publicly. Products cover your high-ticket consulting offers, advisory retainers, or business services, while Promotion outlines the strategic distribution channels you use to share your ideas.

### What are the 7 pillars of personal branding?

The 7 pillars of personal branding include authenticity, expertise, authority, storytelling, relationship building, visual design, and consistent execution. Developing these pillars ensures that your market reputation rests on genuine capability rather than short-lived promotional gimmicks.

When built together, these pillars create an enduring professional presence that transfers seamlessly across multiple companies and ventures over time.

### What is the 3-7-27 rule in branding?

The 3-7-27 rule outlines the three critical windows of human attention. You have three seconds to catch an executive's eye, seven seconds to spark intellectual curiosity, and 27 seconds to communicate your core message.

Applying this pacing to your executive presence means refining your headlines, online bios, and verbal introductions so your authority is felt instantly. Mastering this flow keeps high-level partners engaged before they move on.
