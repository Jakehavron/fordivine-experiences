Common Questions

Is this a sales call?

No. Keep your credit card in your purse for this one.

This call is solely to learn about your situation and see if you're a right fit for our services.

Do I need to have my business fully figured out before working with you?

Not at all.

Many of our clients came to us with a vision they can't quite articulate yet.. and that's exactly what we help with.

Whether you're pivoting, launching something new, or simply outgrowing what you built years ago, we meet you where you are and engineer the brand to amplify where you're going.

What happens during the turn-key brand service?

We review your current brand(s), identify what is unclear or misaligned, then build the complete strategy, messaging, visuals, and systems you will need.

The goal is to make your brand easier to understand, trust, and buy for your ideal audience after you work with us.

What do I walk away with?

Your entire marketing plan and brand assets that are essential to growing your business and impact in 2026 and onward.

This includes your:

  • Crowned Authority™ Blueprint (entire marketing strategy)
  • Crowned Identity™ Bible (all visual + brand assets)
  • Ready-to-use digital assets (content that actually converts)
  • The Engine (systems that work 24/7/365 acquiring new clients/customers)
How much of my time does this require?

Very little. This is primarily a done-for-you service.

Not a video course. Not a coaching program. And not a DIY template.

You show up for a few key conversations and intake forms so we can fully extract your brilliance.

Then we do the rest (marketing strategy, full design, build out, etc).

What are the rates of your services?

Our turn-key personal branding services start at $5k and can be up to $20k+.

It all depends on what your personal brand needs.

If you are a right fit, we will only guide you towards the services that feels mutually aligned.

Note: we do have payment plan financing options.

Is this right for my business or industry?

If your business depends on your name, expertise, reputation, or personal credibility, then our services are made for you.

Our services are not industry-specific, yet they are tailored to where you're current brand journey is at.
