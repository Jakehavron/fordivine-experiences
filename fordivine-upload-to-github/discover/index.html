About FORDIVINE Personal Branding Services

FORDIVINE is a premium personal branding house founded by Emily Ford that helps women entrepreneurs, coaches, speakers, authors, therapists, and leaders build complete brand ecosystems. Through their proprietary Authority Architecture framework, FORDIVINE engineers brands across four layers: Foundation (marketing strategy via the Crowned Authority Blueprint), Presence (visual identity via the Crowned Identity Bible), Activation (digital headquarters and platform presence), and Engine (scalable systems for growth and visibility).

FORDIVINE serves women who have built successful businesses but whose brands no longer match the level of expertise, impact, and authority they have achieved. The service is fully done-for-you, meaning clients receive a complete brand transformation without needing to manage the process themselves.

The discovery call is a complimentary, no-obligation conversation to understand a potential client's brand situation, goals, and whether FORDIVINE's approach is the right fit. It is not a sales call. Rates start at $7,000 for turn-key services. FORDIVINE has served over 36 clients across industries including coaching, consulting, speaking, therapy, fitness, real estate, and wellness.
