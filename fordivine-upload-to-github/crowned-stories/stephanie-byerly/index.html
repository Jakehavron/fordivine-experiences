00The Transformation

From a career in medicine to coaching clients and a TEDx speaking opportunity.

After more than 25 years in medicine, Dr. Stephanie Byerly wanted to help women through coaching and speaking. FORDIVINE helped her connect her experience to a signature message and build a brand that made her next chapter visible.

  • A message she struggled to articulate

01Her Starting Point

Authority in Medicine.
No Authority beyond it.

Stephanie had spent decades caring for patients. Her own experience of healing had also led her toward trauma coaching. She wanted to help women, but struggled to bring that purpose into a clear professional identity.

When she came to FORDIVINE:

  • Her experience had no clear expression online. Scattered posts and an early webinar attempt gave people little sense of her coaching or perspective.
  • Her next chapter lacked direction. She wanted to build beyond her medical role, but previous workshops and mentoring had not given her the brand she needed.
  • Speaking outreach was going unanswered. Without a defined message and professional materials, it was difficult for organizers to see where she fit.
  • She questioned whether her voice belonged. Accomplished in medicine, she felt new to entrepreneurship and unsure how to put herself forward.

After discovering Emily’s content, Stephanie came to FORDIVINE ready to give that next chapter a clear direction.

02The Desired Outcomes

Three goals gave the work direction.

We brought Stephanie’s professional experience, personal perspective, and ambitions together around three priorities.

  1. 1

    Establish a distinct voice beyond medicine.

    Give Stephanie a message and signature methodology that made her perspective recognizable and helped people understand what she could teach.

  2. 2

    Make her expertise clear to event organizers.

    Build a coherent speaking direction and professional marketing materials so organizers could understand her message and its relevance to their audience.

  3. 3

    Build a brand that supports her coaching.

    Help women recognize themselves in her message, understand her work, and see a clear next step toward becoming a client.

The Business + Marketing Blueprint

The Art of Becoming UNNUMB

Stephanie’s work in anesthesiology and her personal experience of healing gave her a distinctive lens on emotional numbness. We helped her articulate that connection through The Art of Becoming UNNUMB.

This signature message gave her coaching, content, and speaking a shared foundation. Her experience could now become a body of work people could recognize and seek her out for.

03The Brand Identity

A professional presence with room for her personality.

Stephanie wanted vibrancy, warmth, and a touch of gold. Her identity needed to express that energy while carrying the credibility of an experienced physician and emerging speaker.

  • Vibrant and personal. Reflect the light and expressiveness she wanted to bring into her next chapter.
  • Polished and professional. Balance color and shimmer with a considered presentation suited to clients and event organizers.
  • Consistent and recognizable. Connect her website, content, and speaking materials through one visual identity.

The result gave Stephanie a professional presence she could recognize herself in and feel comfortable using.

04The Brand In Action

Personal Website That Converts

A clear place to discover her work.

Stephanie’s website brought her experience, message, and coaching into one place. Prospective clients and event organizers could understand her work before reaching out, whether they found her through content or a mutual connection.

Activation Social Content

A clear message. A more visible Stephanie.

With a defined message and professional content, Stephanie began showing more of herself online. Women could hear her perspective, understand her coaching, and see the person behind her medical credentials.

05The Results

Real Results.Real Business Outcomes.

As Stephanie began putting her brand into practice, her clearer message helped new audiences and existing followers understand her work. Her content and connections opened conversations with organizers, while her materials helped them assess her expertise.

Stephanie signed paying clients as her message became clearer and more visible.

  • New clients discovered her through her digital presence.
  • Existing followers took action after recognizing themselves in her message.
  • Her coaching became a growing part of the business she wanted to build.

Stephanie secured an upcoming TEDx speaking opportunity, a significant step toward the public platform she had wanted to build.

  • Her signature message gave her a foundation for developing the talk.
  • Her branding and materials helped make her expertise visible to organizers.

Stephanie was asked to be a main keynote speaker at a women’s medical professional conference in Dallas.

  • The opportunity connects her medical background with the message she now teaches.
  • Organizers could see her perspective through her content, website, and speaking materials.

Stephanie began creating more content and speaking with greater conviction about the work she wanted to do.

  • She moved beyond static posts to show more of herself and her perspective.
  • Her signature message gave her a clear direction for what to share.
  • Her growing visibility also brought podcast interest and invitations.
