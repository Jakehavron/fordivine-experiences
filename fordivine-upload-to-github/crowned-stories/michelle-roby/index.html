01Before the Crown

An established business. An underdeveloped personal brand.

Michelle had built Electric Rays to 10 locations. Behind that accomplishment was a woman still questioning how her experience could translate into a public brand of her own.

When she came to FORDIVINE:

  • Her expertise was difficult to discover. Her personal social presence consisted largely of older family photographs, with little about her business knowledge or leadership.
  • Trust depended heavily on knowing her personally. As her business expanded, she wanted prospective employees, clients, and partners to understand who was leading it.
  • Her next chapter lacked a clear shape. She wanted to help other business owners, but needed to define how her experience could become a consulting offer.
  • She questioned whether people would seek her out. Speaking interested her, but being recognized as a professional speaker still felt unfamiliar.

She brought substantial experience. What she needed was a clear way to communicate it and begin putting it to use beyond her existing businesses.

02The Unveiling

Three goals gave the work direction.

We began by drawing out Michelle’s experience, ambitions, and approach to business. That work helped us connect what she already knew with the opportunities she wanted to pursue.

  1. 1

    Build trust beyond her physical presence.

    Michelle wanted people to understand her leadership without needing a personal introduction. We needed to make the woman behind Electric Rays more visible through clear positioning, messaging, and a consistent public presence.

  2. 2

    Turn her experience into a consulting offer.

    She wanted to help other business owners. We worked to organize her knowledge into signature methods and explain whom she could help, what she could help them with, and how to work with her.

  3. 3

    Be considered for speaking opportunities.

    Michelle wanted to share her knowledge with a wider audience. Her brand needed to make her expertise relevant to event organizers and give them a clear way to inquire.

The Business + Marketing Blueprint

Your Brand Positioned In The White Space

We know how to extract the Brand DNA of who Michelle is while also researching the entire market so she can be authentically herself while not getting lost in the sea of noise or looking like everyone else.

This is how you own your space as a Crowned Authority.

03Building the Crown

A brand that felt like Michelle.

The visual direction needed to reflect Michelle’s taste and the relationships she wanted to build. We translated that brief into an identity with three priorities:

  • Polished and luxurious. Reflect her personal style and give her expertise a considered, professional presentation.
  • Warm and approachable. Preserve the humanity of a leader who cares about people and wants to help other owners.
  • Personal and recognizable. Carry a consistent expression of Michelle across her brand materials and website, so each touchpoint feels connected to the same woman.

These choices gave the creative work a clear purpose. The resulting identity needed to feel natural to Michelle and make sense to the people she hoped to reach.
