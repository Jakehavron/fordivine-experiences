Our Story

A woman with a vision to rebrand the world of personal branding.

01

Emily Ford started FORDIVINE, a personal branding house + method because she saw a massive problem: too many purpose-driven women were building businesses without building personal brands that were authentic to who they truly are. Women unable to "keep up" with their social strategy because it lacked depth, honesty and real value. All while leaving influence, income, and impact on the table.

After scaling her own brands, leading in the wellness space, and mentoring hundreds of entrepreneurs, Emily knew how to bridge the gap between identity and visibility. FORDIVINE exists to help you not just market yourself, but become the woman God always called you to be—bold, branded, and fully aligned. This is where strategy meets Heaven's blueprint and your personal brand becomes the launchpad for legacy.

02

Emily Ford

Host of Called & Crowned | Global Speaker | Personal Branding Expert

Emily Ford built her first business at 23 and grew her wellness company to thousands of customers by age 26 with no personal brand and just under 900 Facebook friends. Since then, she has led organizations across multiple industries, building teams in countries like Australia, the United Kingdom, and South Korea. But it wasn't until she chose to take her personal brand seriously that everything changed.

She realized that a brand built with Heaven's blueprint would allow her to expand her reach, create deeper impact, and do it without compromising the values she holds sacred. That's when doors of opportunity began to open. Features in Forbes three different times, interviews on KTLA, keynote talks in stadiums with 15,000+ attendees, and even new strategic business partnerships.

But beyond the platforms and press, Emily's deepest transformation came through seasons of personal reinvention as she has encountered multiple tragic seasons in her life, both professionally and personally. They were seasons that could have easily broken her down to not continue onward. But instead, these trials are what allowed her to be broken open. It was in that transformational revelation that she felt what so many other women silently feel:
